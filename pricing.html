Frequently asked questions

Can I pay in Nepali Rupees (NPR)? +
Yes! We accept payments via eSewa and Khalti, Nepal's most popular digital wallets. All prices displayed in NPR use the current USD/NPR exchange rate (~133 NPR per USD). You'll see the exact NPR amount before confirming payment.
How do eSewa and Khalti payments work? +
After selecting your plan, choose eSewa or Khalti as your payment method. Enter your registered mobile number, receive an OTP, and confirm. The amount is debited from your digital wallet. You'll receive a payment confirmation email and your subscription activates instantly.
What happens when I exceed my plan limits? +
On the free tier, services will be paused until the next billing cycle. On paid plans, you'll get usage-based overages charged at transparent per-unit rates. We'll notify you before you're charged.
Is there a free trial for paid plans? +
Pro projects come with a 14-day trial. No payment required to start. Team plans include a 30-day trial when initiated from a Pro project.
Can I self-host Nexora? +
Yes. Nexora is 100% open source under the Apache 2.0 license. You can deploy the full stack with Docker Compose or our Helm chart for Kubernetes. The community edition has no feature restrictions.
How does billing work for organizations? +
Billing is per-project on Free and Pro. Team plan is a flat monthly fee per organization, covering all projects. Enterprise is custom. All plans support annual billing with a 20% discount.
